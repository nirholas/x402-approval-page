When the agent needs a grown-up

An agent hits its spending cap, opens an approval request, and sends a person a link. The human pays with their own wallet — that payment is the approval — and the agent fetches the signed grant.

The flow

1 · ASK

Agent

POST /requests — free. It gets back a link and a poll URL.

2 · SEE

Human

Opens the link. Sees who is asking, for what, how much, and why.

3 · PAY

Human

Approves through the modal — Base or Solana, their wallet, their choice.

4 · READ

Agent

GET /requests/:id — $0.001 a look. Signed grant + on-chain receipt.

Why the payment is the approval

A separate "approved" flag would leave two things to reconcile: a decision, and money that may or may not have moved. Here there is one event. The human's payment settles on-chain, and the same response hands back a signed grant — decision, amount, merchant, timestamp, transaction hash — that the agent can present as proof a person authorized this specific purchase for this specific amount.

The checkout is @three-ws/x402-payment-modal, loaded from the CDN. It reads the dual-rail 402 and drives Phantom for Solana or an EVM wallet for Base. Its SIWX re-entry means someone who approves several of these a day signs in once instead of every time, and its client-side spending caps give the human the same kind of guardrail the agent has.
